## GUÍA DE ESTILO VISUAL NOA

### Filosofía visual
Los vídeos de NOA no parecen publicidad. Parecen algo que una madre encontró por casualidad y no puede parar de ver. El estilo visual refuerza esa sensación: hogareño, real, sin artificiosidad. La belleza viene de la autenticidad, no de la producción.

### Paleta de color
```
COLORES PRIMARIOS:
- Blanco roto / crema: #F5F0E8 — fondos principales, calma, claridad
- Terracota cálida: #C25C3A — palabras clave, highlight emocional, energia
- Gris carbón: #2C2C2C — texto en pantalla, seriedad sin frialdad

COLORES SECUNDARIOS:
- Verde salvia: #7A9E7E — momentos de alivio, esperanza, naturaleza
- Beige oscuro: #B8A898 — transiciones, fondos secundarios
- Azul noche: #1A2744 — momentos de peso emocional, noches, profundidad

NUNCA USAR:
- Neón de ningún tipo
- Naranja brillante
- Rosa chicle
- Púrpura saturado
- Verde lima

TEMPERATURA DE COLOR POR TIPO DE ESCENA:
- Momentos de conflicto: tono frío, azulado, bajo contraste
- Momentos de comprensión: neutro-cálido, temperatura media
- Momentos de alivio/CTA: cálido, terracota suave, luz natural
```

### Composición
```
- Proporción: 9:16 siempre (Reels/TikTok)
- Zona segura de texto: 15% de margen en todos los lados
- Texto en pantalla: centrado o un tercio inferior
- B-roll: planos medios y detalles. Nunca plano americano de actor mirando a cámara.
- Profundidad de campo: difuminado de fondo preferente. El detalle debe estar en primer plano.
- Movimiento de cámara: lento, orgánico. Sin efectos de cámara bruscos. Sin zoom rápido.
```

### Estado de luz por escena
```
TENSIÓN / CONFLICTO:
- Luz natural indirecta, gris, sin sol directo
- Ventanas con luz difusa de exterior nublado
- Interior oscuro con única fuente de luz artificial

COMPRENSIÓN / GIRO:
- Transición de frío a cálido en la misma toma
- Contraluz suave que define silueta sin revelar cara

ALIVIO / RESOLUCIÓN:
- Luz dorada de atardecer o de lámpara cálida
- Tonos terracota y dorados en exposición
- Sombras suaves, sin contraste duro
```

### Movimiento
```
- Velocidad de movimiento de cámara: lento (máximo 5% de la resolución por frame)
- Tipo preferido: dolly lento hacia adelante, o tilt muy suave
- Duración de cada clip de b-roll: 3-6 segundos
- Transiciones entre clips: corte limpio (no fundido para la mayoría)
- Fundido a negro: solo para pausas de peso emocional (máx 1 por vídeo)
```

### CATEGORÍA 1 — ESCENAS DEL HOGAR

```
PROMPT-VIS-001 — Cocina tarde, madre quieta
Scene: A Spanish mother in her early 40s stands alone in a modern kitchen in the late afternoon, 
holding a mug of coffee that has gone cold. She stares out the window at the grey sky. 
The kitchen is lived-in, not perfect. A school bag is visible in the background.
Emotion: quiet exhaustion, stillness before the storm.
Lighting: cool natural light from a cloudy sky through the window, warm artificial light from above 
creating a soft contrast.
Movement: very slow dolly forward, 4 seconds.
Color grade: slightly desaturated, cool tones, no harsh shadows.
Duration: 5 seconds.
Negative prompts: no smiling, no posed expressions, no stock photo feel, no perfect kitchen.
```

```
PROMPT-VIS-002 — Puerta de habitación cerrada, pasillo
Scene: A hallway in a Spanish apartment. A closed bedroom door with warm light showing under the gap. 
The hallway light is off. A mother's hand is visible resting on the doorframe, not knocking, just 
standing there.
Emotion: hesitation, wanting to connect but not knowing how.
Lighting: strong contrast between the dark hallway and the light under the door.
Movement: static shot, slight breathing motion.
Color grade: cool hallway, warm crack of light as the only color warmth.
Duration: 4 seconds.
Negative prompts: no faces visible, no dialogue, no posed gestures.
```

```
PROMPT-VIS-003 — Deberes sin hacer, mesa de estudio
Scene: Close-up of a school desk. An open notebook with only one or two written lines, a pencil 
lying sideways, a math worksheet untouched. An empty chair. Clock on the wall showing 7:15 PM.
Emotion: abandonment, time pressure, the weight of unfinished things.
Lighting: single warm desk lamp. Rest of room in shadow.
Movement: very slight pan across the desk surface, 3 seconds.
Color grade: warm lamp vs cool room background.
Duration: 4 seconds.
Negative prompts: no child visible, no cartoonish props, no exaggerated mess.
```

```
PROMPT-VIS-004 — Mochila tirada en la entrada
Scene: A teenager's school backpack dropped on the floor in the entrance of an apartment, 
right inside the door. Keys are still in the lock. Shoes kicked off. A jacket half-on the hook.
Emotion: urgency, arrival in chaos, the explosion that just happened.
Lighting: afternoon light from door left open, casting long shadows inside.
Movement: camera starts on backpack and slowly pulls back to reveal the whole entrance.
Color grade: warm outside light, cool interior.
Duration: 4 seconds.
Negative prompts: no people visible, no staged scene.
```

```
PROMPT-VIS-005 — Madre en sofá, tarde noche
Scene: A woman in her 40s sitting on a sofa in a dim living room, late evening. 
Her phone is face down on the cushion next to her. She's not watching TV. 
Her eyes are open but unfocused. One hand holds her forehead lightly.
Emotion: mental exhaustion after an emotionally heavy day. Not crying, just depleted.
Lighting: single floor lamp at low brightness, warm but limited. TV light off.
Movement: completely static. Shot holds for full duration.
Color grade: very warm but very dim. Slight vignette.
Duration: 5 seconds.
Negative prompts: no dramatic crying, no phone in use, no TV playing.
```

### CATEGORÍA 2 — TENSIÓN FAMILIAR

```
PROMPT-VIS-006 — Silueta de adolescente cerrando puerta
Scene: Silhouette of a teenager (15-16 years old, cannot see face) walking down a hallway 
and entering their room. The door closes firmly but not quite slamming. From the perspective 
of someone standing at the far end of the hall.
Emotion: disconnection, the end of a conversation that didn't happen.
Lighting: backlit from the room doorway. Hallway completely dark.
Movement: camera static, subject moves naturally, door closes.
Color grade: high contrast silhouette. Almost no detail in the figure.
Duration: 4 seconds.
Negative prompts: no slamming animation, no dramatic gesture, no face visible.
```

```

distant. No eye contact. No conversation. A distance between them even though they're touching 
the same table.
Emotion: parallel solitude, coexistence without connection.
Lighting: overhead kitchen light. Neutral, functional, no warmth.
Movement: slow push-in from far, keeping both in frame.
Color grade: neutral, slightly flat.
Duration: 5 seconds.
Negative prompts: no angry expressions, no dramatic body language. Subtlety required.
```

```

No movement. Silence captured in stillness.
Emotion: standoff, the moment before something is said or left unsaid.
Lighting: overhead light. Hard shadows from hands on table surface.
Movement: completely static. The tension is in the stillness.
Color grade: slightly cool, high contrast hands vs table.
Duration: 3 seconds.
Negative prompts: no raised hands, no pointing, no extreme close-up of fingernails only.
```

```

A moment of hesitation before something happens.
Emotion: threshold moment, the pause before re-engaging.
Lighting: morning light from a window at top of stairs. Warm backlight.
Movement: the teenager stops mid-stair. Camera stays static.
Color grade: warm morning tones, slight lens flare from window.
Duration: 4 seconds.
Negative prompts: no dramatic running down, no extreme expressions.
```

```
PROMPT-VIS-010 — Teléfono móvil en la cama, madrugada

Movement: raindrops move naturally down the glass. Figure completely still.

```

Emotion: seeking comfort in small things, a pause in a difficult day.
Lighting: soft warm window light from the side. Golden hour or overcast warm interior.
Movement: very slight steam movement. Hands completely still.
Color grade: warm ochre tones, slight haze from steam.
Duration: 4 seconds.

```
PROMPT-VIS-013 — Cuaderno con escritura a mano

Lighting: warm desk lamp. Shallow focus on the handwriting.
Movement: very slow pan across the pages from right to left.
Color grade: warm paper tones, slightly vintage feel.
Duration: 4 seconds.
Negative prompts: no visible faces, no specific readable diagnostic words.
```

```
PROMPT-VIS-014 — Reloj de cocina, tarde

Movement: clock hand ticking. Camera completely static.
Color grade: neutral kitchen tones. Nothing stylized.

Negative prompts: no people in frame, no dramatic close-up of clock face only.

```
PROMPT-VIS-015 — Almohada en la oscuridad, noche
Scene: A pillow in a dimly lit bedroom at night. The bed is half-made, as if someone

Empty room but with the presence of someone who was just there.
Emotion: sleeplessness, the 3 AM thoughts of a parent, the weight of worry.
